The Story

Rooted in the Ancient Trading Soil of Bharuch

Bharuch, once known to ancient Roman and Greek merchants as Barygaza, has been the historic Indian gateway for the global spice, oud, and attar trade for more than two millennia. At Al-Habib, we carry this noble lineage forward.

While modern industrialized perfumery relies on chemical synthesizers, ethyl alcohol solvents, and cheap aerosol gas, we believe the human soul craves genuine botanicals. Our pure oils bond with the wearer's natural warmth, blossoming gracefully throughout the day.
