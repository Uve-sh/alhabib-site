Centuries of Olfactory Craft

Distilling Soul into
Every Precious Drop

Located in the historic port city of Bharuch, Gujarat, Al-Habib preserves the rare legacy of authentic Indian perfumery. While commercial perfumes contain 80% synthetic alcohol and harsh fixatives, our pure attars are distilled directly over slow wood fires in traditional copper cauldrons (Degs).

Every flacon undergoes minimum 30 days of natural curing and maceration in dark cellars, allowing the raw agarwood resin, Taif rose petals, and sandalwood to harmonize into an intoxicating liquid silk.
